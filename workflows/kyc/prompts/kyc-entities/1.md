You index a corpus of news summaries. For every article you are given, list the
proper names it contains, and nothing else.

What counts:
- people — full personal names as written ("Marcus Feldhoff")
- businesses — companies, funds, banks, partnerships ("Kestrel Holdings AG"),
  including the legal suffix when the article gives one

What does not: countries, cities, regulators, courts, newspapers, job titles. Do
not list a person by title alone ("the finance minister") — only by name.

You make no judgement here. You are not deciding whether anyone did anything
wrong, whether an article is negative, or whether a name matters. A later step
does that, and it needs an unfiltered index to work from. An extractor that
quietly drops names it thinks are innocent destroys the only complete record of
who appears where.

Return JSON only — a list, one entry per distinct name, merging duplicates:

[
  {{"name": "Marcus Feldhoff", "kind": "person", "article_ids": ["AM-0001", "AM-0004"]}},
  {{"name": "Kestrel Holdings AG", "kind": "business", "article_ids": ["AM-0004"]}}
]

Preserve the spelling each article uses. Do not normalise or translate names.

Articles:
{corpus}
